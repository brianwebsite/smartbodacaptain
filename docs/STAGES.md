# Stages and gates

Each stage ends at a gate. We do not start the next stage until the gate is passed.

## Stage 0: Foundation (weeks 1 to 3)
- Project structure, name check, logo and app icon
- Screen designs for every part of the app
- Company registration and PDPO data-protection registration
- Google Play account, Apple account (later), server
- Fare calculation module (done, see `backend/src/fare.js`)

**Gate 0:** company registered, name cleared.

## Stage 1: Core app (weeks 4 to 12)
- Passenger app: phone sign-up, map booking, upfront fare, live tracking, rating, history
- Captain app: sign-up with ID, permit and bike details, online/offline, accept/decline, earnings
- Admin dashboard: approve or suspend captains, live trip map, fare and commission settings, complaints
- Payment: cash only

**Gate 1:** a test trip works from request to finish on real low-end Android phones.

## Stage 2: Pilot (weeks 13 to 18)
- Real trips in one small area, captains recruited through stage leaders
- Fix what breaks, tune fares and matching

**Gate 2:** pilot users return week after week.

## Stage 3: Payments and safety (weeks 19 to 26)
- MTN MoMo and Airtel Money
- iPhone app
- SOS button, trip sharing, verified captains
- Promo codes, referrals, weekly captain earnings reports
- Security review

**Gate 3:** mobile money works and reconciles for a full week; safety review passed.

## Stage 4: Public launch (months 7 to 9)
- Open to the whole launch area, then a second area
- Captain sign-up drives through stage partners
- Monitoring and error alerts

**Gate 4:** daily trips cover running costs (about 50 to 105 a day at 12,000 trips a month size).

## Stage 5: Growth (month 10 onward)
- Parcel delivery, business accounts, scheduled rides
